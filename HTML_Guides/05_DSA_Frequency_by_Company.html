🏛️ For Captive Centers (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Optum, Carelon)

1
Week 1: Master Q1–50 (Foundations) — Arrays, Strings, Hashing, Math
2
Week 2: Master Q51–90 (Sorting, Searching, Stacks, LinkedLists)
3
Week 3: Review Q1–90 twice, focus on edge cases
4
Practice: 5–10 Easy problems daily on LeetCode
5
Skip: Q121–180 unless targeting senior architect roles

🚀 For Product Companies (Flipkart, Razorpay, PayTM, Swiggy, CRED)

1
Week 1–2: Master Q1–50 (must be fast, < 15 min each)
2
Week 3–4: Master Q51–120 (Sliding Window + Binary Search are key)
3
Week 5–6: Master Q121–180 (Trees + DP are most asked)
4
Practice: 3–5 Medium problems daily + 1 Hard per week
5
Mock: 2–3 mock interviews per week from week 4
