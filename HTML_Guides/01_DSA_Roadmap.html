Phase 1: Foundations

Week 1–2 · Q1–50 · 15 hours · Arrays, Strings, Hashing, Math

Tier 1 START HERE
Arrays (Q1–Q15)Reverse, rotate, duplicates, missing, Kadane's
Strings (Q16–Q30)Palindrome, anagram, sliding window, frequency
Hashing (Q31–Q40)Two Sum, Three Sum, two pointers, valid parentheses
Math (Q41–Q50)Fibonacci, prime, factorial, GCD, power
Why start here? 80% of captive center interviews (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Optum, Carelon, Deloitte) are covered by Q1–50. These are your bread-and-butter questions. Master them first.

Week 3–4 · Q51–120 · 20 hours · Sorting, Searching, Stacks, LinkedLists

Tier 1–2
